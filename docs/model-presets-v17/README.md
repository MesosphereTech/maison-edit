# V17 adult model presets

Claire (32) and Julian (34) are original fictional adult models created with the built-in image-generation tool. The original everyday woman (45), relaxed man (50) and all additional selectable persona examples remain available. New visitors start with Claire and English; saved language and case choices remain recoverable.

## What was actually generated

- Two consistent, neutral full-body identity portraits
- Five Claire full outfits: walnut tailoring, camel coat, terracotta dress, oat knit/champagne skirt, and cropped oat trench/pale-blue knit
- Two Julian full outfits: ink tailoring and oat fine-rib knit
- Nine optimized production WebP files total, 1,295,044 bytes

The catalog's actual source pixels were supplied as references. Full outfits include the primary-color catalog apparel, matching shoes and appropriate accessories. Each new identity has continuous face, hair and build across its scenes. All nine outputs were visually checked for adult identity, face, anatomy, head-to-toe framing, apparel silhouettes and colors. These remain synthetic styling concepts, not exact physical-product photography, verified measurements or fit predictions.

## Honest coverage

Claire has 13 mapped fashion pieces across five distinct full-outfit images. Julian has seven mapped fashion pieces across two distinct images. Several products legitimately share a complete outfit image. This is 20 SKU-to-outfit mappings, not 20 independently generated images.

The original 56 SKU scenes remain unchanged for the older two cases. They all include those original adults and are never assigned to the new models. Home pieces and unsupported accessories in new cases display the original product concept with an explicit fallback label. Opposite-department apparel uses the other new adult model through the existing gift-model presentation and selected packaging preview.

Each of four cases has independent saved profile, history, wishlist, cart and feedback. Fictional clothing/height/shoe templates are Claire S/175 cm/EU39, Julian L/186 cm/EU43, everyday woman M/165 cm/EU38, and relaxed man L/172 cm/EU42. Templates are declared examples, never image-derived measurements.

## Provenance and validation

`provenance.json` records production dimensions, byte counts, SHA-256 hashes, source-reference hashes, exact prompts, pictured SKU/color mappings and the limits of all claims. `generation-prompts.json` retains the compact prompt set. Raw generation PNGs and catalog reference boards are retained under ignored `artifacts/model-presets-v17/`, outside the production public directory and source commit.

`tests/model-presets.test.mjs` covers four-case ordering/defaults, independent templates and persisted choices, every gender-matched apparel anchor, exact colors, original-image fallback, gift model and wrap routing, matching visit/guide identities and all nine production byte hashes. Legacy scene and ownership regressions use an explicitly selected v16 warm fixture, preserving their original scope while the new defaults have dedicated coverage.
