# MAISON ÉDIT v16 review

Built from v15 commit `203ab08d1554890f4bcd9ebd2c340a750fba30f3` in the same Sites project. The previous dirty workspace was left untouched. No push or deployment has been performed.

## What changed

- Visible product images acknowledge entry immediately. The current piece gets a stable outline and its local catalog bubble is readable before the decorative border lap or pixel handoff finishes.
- Image visibility is measured from the image, not the trailing product metadata. Offscreen sources, stale hover timers and superseded events are canceled. Closing the companion stays closed through scrolling and incoming copy.
- A real visible inset teaches image swapping with an illustrated hand, short bilingual instructions, keyboard support and durable per-case tab dismissal. No auto-swap or focus theft.
- Template selection now has one clear action, a truthful same-screen loading state, and direct store entry. Only two fictional sets are supported. Retry, cancel, per-case state and music are preserved.
- An empty visit page now has explorable, clearly labeled curated itineraries, exact catalog pieces, home/gift stops and a fictional floor route. Examples never create a booking or change the shopper's list. Actual saved bookings, preparation, alternatives and cancellation remain available.
- The visit companion/input and case/music controls have reserved document space. They do not float over the hero or its actions. The existing audio instance remains mounted.
